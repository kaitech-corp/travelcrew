## What is Travel Crew

Travel Crew is a mobile app for friends to plan, organize, and experience trips together. Create full itineraries, coordinate with your crew, vote on lodging and activities, split expenses, and stay connected through group chat — all in one place.

## Features

- **Trip Management** — Create and manage trips with destination, dates, privacy settings, and cover photos
- **Crew Collaboration** — Invite friends, follow other travelers, and coordinate details together
- **Activities** — Add and vote on activities for the trip
- **Flights & Lodging** — Track airline info, flight numbers, hotels, and check-in/out dates
- **Expense Splitting** — Log shared expenses and compute optimal settlements with minimum transfers
- **Group Chat** — Per-trip chat rooms for real-time coordination
- **Notifications** — Stay updated on trip activity across your crew
- **Import from AI** — Plan a trip in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any AI assistant, then import directly into TravelCrew with a single paste

## Import from AI

TravelCrew doesn't try to replace AI trip planning — it bridges the gap between AI-generated itineraries and real group coordination.

**How it works:**
1. Open My Trips and tap the ✨ icon
2. Copy the TravelCrew prompt and paste it into any AI assistant
3. Describe your trip in the chat, then ask the AI to format the output
4. Paste the JSON response back into TravelCrew
5. Review the pre-filled trip details and tap Create

The AI response populates destination, dates, airline info, lodging, and a full activities list — all editable before saving.
